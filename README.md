# Lyrics-Generator
Lyrics Generator with **Remotion** and **GroqCloud Whisper API** *[Free for Fair Use]*

## Quick Start
• Go to the [Actions](https://github.com/gabrihh/Lyrics-Generator/actions/workflows/render.yml) tab.
• Click on **"Run workflow"**.
• Define the following inputs:<br>
**| Audio URL (.mp3)** - Your music at URL<br>
**| Song title** - The song title will appear in the top left corner.<br>
**| Artist / Author** - The author of the song will appear below the title.<br>
**| Language (optional)** - It makes AI-powered transcription faster and more efficient.<br>
**| Background color (hex)** - Background image in hex code.<br>
**| Logo URL (optional)** - The logo will appear in the bottom right corner.<br>
**| Lyric Example** - Add snippets of music and improve the AI's accuracy.<br>
